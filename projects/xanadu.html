Secure information system for Xanadu

A case study: modernize and secure the information system of a fictional company moving its headquarters to Atlantis and opening a remote site in Springfield. We mapped the network, servers and directory, set filtering and backup policies, automated administration with PowerShell and Group Policy, and built a working mock-up.
